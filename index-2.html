Risk, return, and valuation calculators

Measure what a stock actually did, and how risky it was

This site turns a stock's price history into the numbers investors use to judge it: returns, volatility, beta, alpha, correlation, and risk-adjusted performance. A second calculator covers market capitalization, P/E, dividend yield, and book-to-market.

Open Financial Analysis, paste prices (or use the sample data), and press Calculate. Every result shows the formula behind it.
